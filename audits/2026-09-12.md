## Observations

1. **Every signal is tracking-only — the bot placed nothing today.** `back_probe_signals` equals `signals_total` (35/35), meaning the probe-hold or per-signal probe flags are firing on 100% of output, including all three lay signals. Whether this reflects a still-active global probe hold or a systematic per-signal trigger is not determinable from this data alone. The practical consequence is zero operator action was possible regardless of signal quality. Confidence: **high** (the numbers are unambiguous; the *cause* of the 100% probe rate is uncertain). This is operationally worth checking: if a probe hold was meant to be temporary and has been forgotten, it is silently suppressing every signal the bot generates.

2. **Lay drought continues: 3 lays out of 35 signals (8.6%), with zero in the <65% market-prob band.** The three lays that did appear land in the ≥90% (n=2) and 65–89.99% (n=1) bands. *Relation to Established Finding #1:* the project's claimed lay edge lives below 65% implied probability, yet no signal today hit that band. *Relation to Established Finding #2:* two of the three lays are in the ≥90% band, which has a documented 1W/24L live record — the structurally worst lay segment. Today's sample (n=3 lays) is far too small to update the historical picture, but the pattern of zero signals in the proven-edge band and two in the known-losing band is worth tracking. Confidence: **speculative** (n=3 lays on one day; directional consistency with prior drought data, not a new finding).

3. **Ten resolutions at 10.0% WR, −£12.69 — well below any profitable baseline.** The breakdown: backs 7 resolved, 1 win, 14.3% WR, −£9.45; lays 3 resolved, 0 wins, 0.0% WR, −£3.24. Single-day resolution counts are not informative — n=10 total, n=1 per lay band, is essentially noise — but the consecutive-losses counter was pinned at 3 across all seven runs, confirming the losing streak predates today and did not recover. Confidence: **speculative** (n=10 resolutions; no meaningful update to win-rate estimates).

Confidence: **high** (counts from logs are direct; impact on signal quality is an inference from an established finding).

Confidence: **speculative** (two flags, one ambiguous; insufficient to characterise the false-positive rate).

## Recommended actions

1. **Verify whether a global probe hold is currently active and intentional.** A `back_probe` rate of 100% (35/35 signals, including lays) on a normal trading day is either a still-live hold flag that should have been reverted, or a systematic probe trigger applying to every signal. Check `config/config.toml` → `thresholds.global_probe_hold` and the per-sport probe config. If the hold was temporary and has been left on, revert it; if it is intentional, document why so future audits can discount the "zero placeable signals" reading.

2. **Keep monitoring the lay-band distribution once n≥15 lay signals accumulates (post-probe-hold).** Today's 8.6% lay rate and zero signals in the sub-65% band are consistent with the documented lay drought, but at n=3 lays no threshold adjustment is warranted. Once a sustained post-hold window provides 15+ lay signals spread across market-probability bands, re-examine whether the sub-65% band is being populated at all — if it continues to show near-zero representation, the market-selection or probability-calculation step may be structurally biased away from the claimed edge band.

## Caveats

All resolution figures (n=10 total, n=1–3 per sub-segment) are noise-level for any probability estimate. A single day's WR of 10% is consistent with both a genuinely broken strategy and a random bad day on a 60–70% WR strategy — roughly a 1-in-1000 outcome but not ruled out by a sample this small. The CLV sidecar logged zero headline-eligible entries today, so there is no independent price-movement check on signal quality. The consecutive-losses counter at 3 all day indicates a streak predating this window but gives no information about its length or significance without the full tracker history. Any pattern noted above would need to replicate across a minimum of 30–50 resolved trades before supporting a config or threshold change.
