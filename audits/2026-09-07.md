Research fallback activated 14 times, with Claude Code sessions covering the shortfall (22/22 groups in the first fallback alone). This is operationally concerning: primary research was unavailable for the bulk of the day. Combined with established finding #4 (web research contributes ~0.13pp of edge on average), the practical signal-quality impact of running on fallback is probably small — but the pattern of hitting the daily cap on run 1 suggests the quota is structurally too tight for the current schedule (7 runs/day × batch sizes shown), and is worth addressing before it degrades to a day with no fallback coverage.

2. **Zero Tavily credits used; 70% of researched markets served from cache.** Of 377 markets researched, 265 were cache hits and 0 Tavily API credits were consumed. This is consistent with established finding #4 — that Tavily/web research contributes an average of ~0.13pp to a signal's stated edge. On a day where no fresh external searches were paid for, the model's signals rest almost entirely on cached data, the Wang transform, calibration bands, and the unexplained/artifact component identified in the original diagnosis. That is probably the norm rather than an exception, but it directly undercuts any claim that the 24 today's signals reflect live-updated research. Confidence: high (the credit count of 0 and cache count of 265 are unambiguous). Relation to finding #4: consistent with it, and arguably strengthens it — if research is routinely 70%+ cached and credits are not being spent, the 0.13pp contribution figure may be an overestimate of what today's signals reflect.

3. **All 3 lay signals land in the 65–89.99% market-probability band; none in the <65% proven-edge band.** The data shows `by_lay_market_prob_band: {"65-89.99%": {"n": 3}}` with no other lay-band entries. Confidence: high that the signals are in this band; speculative on what the outcomes mean at n=2. Relation to findings #1 and #2: today's lay signal generation is misaligned with the historically profitable band; no signals were generated in the <65% zone at all.

The live loss streak at 6 is therefore a persistent open item not addressed by today's activity. Confidence: medium (inference from the unchanged counter; the counter's exact update logic is not confirmed from the data alone).

## Recommended actions

1. **Investigate and address the Gemini daily-quota exhaustion.** With 19 x 429 errors hitting before 06:10 UTC on a 7-run day, the primary research provider is structurally unreliable under the current schedule. Before this becomes a day with zero fallback coverage, it is worth either (a) raising the Gemini quota tier, (b) reducing batch sizes per run, or (c) treating Claude Code fallback as the promoted primary with Gemini demoted to top-up. This is actionable now; it does not require waiting for more trade data.

2. **Confirm the current state of `global_probe_hold` and document it.** The 23/24 probe classification is either expected (if the project-wide hold is still active) or a signal that per-sport probe rules are now effectively suppressing the entire book. Either way, the operator should have a clear, auditable answer to "how many real placeable signals per day is the bot currently generating?" — today's answer appears to be at most 1. If the probe hold has been on longer than intended, the open live-loss streak of 6 is accumulating without any live resolution opportunities to reverse it.

## Caveats

All resolution figures today are n=6 (4 backs, 2 lays). At this sample size, no single-day win-rate or P&L figure should be used to confirm or contradict any band-level finding — a 100% WR on 2 lays is entirely consistent with both +EV and -EV expectations. The consecutive-losses streak interpretation (observation #5) is an inference; the data does not directly expose the counter's update logic or which specific trades constitute the streak. The Gemini fallback conclusion (observation #1) is high-confidence on the error pattern but cannot assess whether the Claude Code fallback's output quality is equivalent to the primary — that would require a separate content audit. For any of these observations to carry real evidential weight, the relevant segments would need n≥30 resolved trades under consistent conditions (same probe/live classification, same edge band, same provider).
