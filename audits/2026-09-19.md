# Research Audit — 2026-09-19

## Summary

All 7 scheduled routine runs completed with clean exits, and the pipeline is nominally healthy. However, the day's signal picture is dominated by two structural facts that dwarf the daily P&L: 92 of 93 signals (98.9%) carry the back_probe flag, meaning effectively the entire signal output is tracking-only and nothing is genuinely placeable; and every single unit of research input (249/249 cache hits, 0 Tavily credits consumed) came from stale cached sources rather than fresh live web searches. Lay performance on the day was poor (16.7% WR, -£1.86 on n=6), but the sample is far too small to say anything useful about it. The more important questions are whether the near-universal probe flag is intentional and whether the zero-fresh-research condition is affecting signal quality.

## Observations

1. **98.9% of signals are back_probe — the bot may be in a de-facto full hold.** 92 of 93 signals generated today carry `back_probe=true`. The field notes for this dataset explicitly flag that if back_probe appears on effectively all signals including lays, "that is a real, checkable pattern worth surfacing, not a labelling artefact." With 8 lay signals generated but only 15 reaching the Telegram slip across all 7 runs (and 7 of those 15 slots likely going to probe-tagged signals), the gap between signals generated and signals the operator could actually place is close to total. It is not possible from this data alone to determine whether the project-wide `global_probe_hold` flag is still set or whether the per-sport probe mechanism is cascading this broadly — but the pattern is identical to what a global hold would produce. Confidence: high as a pattern, speculative as to cause.

2. **Zero fresh research today — all 249 briefs served from cache, 0 Tavily credits used.** Of 378 markets researched, 249 were cache hits and 9 were deferred; the remainder presumably used the Gemini grounding fallback triggered by the 27 × 429 errors (daily cap hit from the first run at 06:03). No Tavily credits were consumed at all. This is directly relevant to established finding #4: that web research contributes only ~0.13 percentage points of stated edge on average. If the research step is running on stale cached content rather than live sources, the already-minimal information contribution is further diminished — but given the measured 0.13pp baseline, the practical signal-quality impact is likely near zero. What it does affect is confidence that the research step is functioning as designed. The Gemini 429 rate (27 hits, 14 fallback activations, first error at 06:03 on the first run of the day) suggests the daily cap is being exhausted in the first run and all subsequent runs fall back — an infrastructure pattern worth checking. Confidence: high on the fact (cache-only), speculative on the consequence.

3. **Today's lay results are poor but statistically meaningless (n=6, 16.7% WR, -£1.86).** Six lays resolved today with one win. Breaking down by the market-probability bands that established finding #1 considers the relevant dimension: the <65% band (the historically profitable zone) went 0/1, -£1.86; the 65-89.99% band went 1/3, +£0.84; the ≥90% band went 0/2, -£0.84. Every one of these sub-counts is n≤3. No inference about any band is possible from a single day at these sizes; this observation is recorded only for completeness. Confidence: speculative — n<15 in every sub-band.

4. **The ≥90% lay band (the known losing segment) produced 0/2 today — consistent with the established finding but adding trivially to the evidence base.** Established finding #2 cites a 1W/24L history on lays against ≥90%-implied favourites. Today's 0/2 on that band is directionally consistent, bringing the theoretical running count to 1W/26L if these two are new entries in that segment. However, n=2 on a single day adds almost nothing to the statistical picture — the finding was already suggestive at n=25 and remains so. The observation is noted as directionally consistent, not as confirmation. Confidence: speculative as a standalone data point; the prior finding remains the load-bearing evidence.

5. **Golf accounts for 63 of 93 signals (67.7%) — concentration remains high despite diversification work.** The category breakdown shows golf producing more than twice the signal volume of all other sports combined. Soccer (5), politics (7), basketball (0), tennis (0), cricket (2), Australian rules (6), darts (3), baseball (4), boxing (2), rugby league (1) together produce 30 signals. No other sport breaks double digits. This is a data point about the model's current output distribution on this particular Saturday, not necessarily a structural problem — golf's signal share is likely calendar-driven (tournament weekend) — but it remains the case that the project's signal base is heavily concentrated in a single sport by volume. Confidence: high as a fact about today; speculative as a structural pattern given calendar effects.

## Recommended actions

1. **Verify whether `global_probe_hold` is still active.** The 92/93 back_probe rate is the most operationally significant number in today's data. If the hold was set as a temporary measure (e.g. during operator absence) and was supposed to be reverted, it may still be live. Check `config/config.toml` → `thresholds.global_probe_hold` and confirm whether the current value is intentional. If it is intentional, no action; if not, this is suppressing all live placement decisions. No other diagnostic step is needed — this is a one-line config read.

2. **Keep monitoring the Gemini 429 exhaustion pattern; no immediate action needed.** The first-run 429s (three consecutive hits at 06:03–06:04) suggest the daily quota is being consumed before the day's first routine completes. With 14 fallback activations across 7 runs and 0 Tavily credits consumed, the research step is operating entirely on cached or fallback content on most runs. Given that fresh research's measured contribution to edge is ~0.13pp, this is unlikely to move the P&L needle materially — but if the intent is that research should provide live data, the quota exhaustion pattern is worth a periodic check to ensure the fallback chain is working as designed and the cache TTL is appropriate for the market types being covered.

## Caveats

All P&L and win-rate figures cited for today (9 resolutions, sub-band counts of n=1 to n=3) are orders of magnitude below any threshold for statistical inference. A single day's trading at £2 flat stake produces noise, not signal. The established project findings referenced here (the lay-edge finding, the ≥90% band loss rate, the calibration equivalence claim, the 0.13pp research contribution) are themselves based on multi-hundred-trade histories that took months to accumulate; a single day can be directionally consistent or inconsistent with them without moving the underlying evidence base in either direction. No finding in this audit should be acted upon solely on the basis of today's numbers. The one recommended action (checking `global_probe_hold`) is a config read that carries no risk; the second is a monitoring note, not a change. The CLV sidecar has not been updated since 2026-09-02 and contains no entries logged on 2026-09-19 — no closing-line-value analysis is possible for this date.
