## Recommended actions

1. **Check whether the project-wide probe hold is still deliberately active.** With 27 of 28 signals tagged `back_probe=true` — including both lay signals — the operator may be receiving slips with zero live-placement candidates each run. A one-line config check (`thresholds.global_probe_hold` in config.toml) confirms whether this is intentional. If the hold has been left on unintentionally past its intended end date, every day it persists is a missed live-placement window; if it is deliberately on, the audit trail is clear.

2. **Tag each signal at generation time with its research provider (primary Gemini vs fallback) and compare stated edge by path once ~30 signals per path accumulate.** The 33 Gemini 429s and 14 fallback activations on a single day suggest this split is routine, not exceptional. Established Finding #4's ~0.13pp research contribution was measured against the primary path; a labelled comparison requires no live-strategy changes, costs nothing, and would close the current monitoring gap on whether fallback research systematically inflates or deflates stated edge relative to the baseline.

## Caveats

All resolution figures are n≤15 total, n≤11 / n≤4 by side, and n≤2 per lay market-probability band. Nothing in the resolution data is statistically meaningful in isolation. The day is directionally consistent with Established Findings #1 and #2 (sub-65% lay band near-breakeven, ≥90% band losing) but n=2 per band adds negligible information to the cumulative record. The consecutive-loss counter at 6 is a multi-day trailing metric. Established Findings #1–#4 rest on hundreds of cumulative trades; a single day's n=15 cannot confirm, contradict, or update them — it can only be noted as consistent or inconsistent, and on this day the data is mostly consistent with the prior conclusions or silent (no CLV, no research-contribution decomposition available for today's specific signals).
