# Research Audit — 2026-09-21

## Summary

A quiet, technically-healthy day: all 7 routine runs completed with clean exits and zero tracebacks. Signal generation produced 28 signals across 7 runs, but 27 of those 28 were flagged `back_probe=true` (tracking-only), making the day operationally near-dormant from a placement perspective. The one area of concern is research infrastructure: the Gemini daily cap was exhausted within the first scheduled run (06:00 UTC), triggering 14 fallback activations across the day and leaving 75% of research sourced from cache. Fourteen trades resolved at a 28.6% win rate and -£5.79 net — a bad day numerically, though the tiny sample prevents any meaningful inference.

## Observations

1. **Effectively all signals are tracking-only.** 27 of 28 signals carry `back_probe=true`, meaning essentially nothing was placeable today. This rate (96.4%) is consistent with the project-wide `global_probe_hold` flag being active, which forces every signal — including lays — into tracking-only mode regardless of sport or edge. If that flag was not intentionally left on, this is an operational gap worth confirming. Even if the hold is intentional, an explicit confirmation that it remains deliberate for this period is warranted given that it has been in place since at least the 2026-09-02 session notes.

2. **Zero lay signals generated in the only proven-edge band (<65% market-implied probability).** The 4 lay signals today sat entirely outside the <65% band — 3 in the 65–89.99% range and 1 in the ≥90% range. The single ≥90% signal resolved as a loss (-£0.12), consistent with the documented 1W/24L record for that specific band (established finding #2). The <65% band, described as the only historically positive lay segment, produced no signals today. This is in tension with established finding #1 ("the only proven edge is lays on selections priced below 65%") in the sense that the pipeline is not generating opportunities in that band — though whether that reflects a structural scarcity or a transient calendar effect is unclear from one day. Confidence: speculative (n=4 lay signals total).

3. **Lay P&L on resolutions was driven by the mid-probability band, not the extreme.** Of 7 resolved lays: the 65–89.99% band went 0/3 at -£3.10, the <65% band went 1/3 at -£1.60, and the ≥90% band went 0/1 at -£0.12. The worst P&L came from the 65–89.99% band — the same band that today generated 3 of the 4 new lay signals. This is consistent with the concern that lays above the 65% threshold are not the profit source, but n=7 resolving lays (across all bands, on a single day) is far too small to draw any conclusion. State: directionally consistent with established finding #1, but evidence strength is low. Confidence: speculative (n=7 lays total, n=3 per sub-band).

4. **Gemini daily cap exhausted within the first routine run.** All three sampled 429 errors are timestamped 06:02–06:03 UTC — the very start of the 06:00 run. A total of 26 rate-limit hits, 13 service-unavailable errors, 14 fallback activations, and one full provider exhaustion were logged across the day. With 75% of research served from cache and 0 Tavily credits used, the majority of today's "research" reflected prior-day data rather than fresh retrieval. This is directly relevant to established finding #4 (~0.13pp average research contribution): if cached/fallback research is systematically lower quality than primary research, the already-near-zero real contribution of the research step could be even smaller on days like this. No way to quantify from this data alone, but it strengthens the case that research is not driving signals. Confidence: high on the infrastructure observation; speculative on the incremental impact to signal quality.

5. **Politics accounted for 43% of all signals (12/28), with Australian Rules contributing 4 signals despite not being a standard configured category.** Politics is allocated 2 quota slots per run under the unified category system, giving a theoretical maximum of 14 politics signals across 7 runs — today achieved 12/14 (86% utilisation), suggesting that politics is consistently generating candidates to fill its quota. Australian Rules (4 signals) is not among the named category slots in the project's current configuration, meaning it reached the slip via remainder-fill logic. No obvious issue here, but the category distribution is unusual relative to prior-period signals and worth a brief check to confirm the remainder-fill path is behaving as intended. Confidence: speculative (one day's snapshot, no cross-day baseline in this data).

## Recommended actions

1. **Confirm whether `global_probe_hold` is still deliberately active.** 27/28 signals being probe-only is the expected pattern when the hold flag is `true`, but the last documented state (CLAUDE.md) described it as a temporary measure while the operator was away. If the hold was supposed to have been reverted and wasn't, the bot has been generating but not surfacing any placeable signals for an indeterminate period. A one-line config check (`grep global_probe_hold config/config.toml`) takes seconds and resolves the ambiguity. No code change needed until the operator's intent is confirmed.

2. **Investigate the Gemini daily-cap pattern before the next trading day.** The cap is being exhausted in the first of seven daily runs (06:00 UTC), which means six subsequent runs operate on fallback or cache. If the cap level is configurable (per-project API quota), raising it or redistributing the batch sizing to throttle early-run usage would improve research freshness across the full day. Given finding #4, this is unlikely to materially improve signal quality — but it does mean the infrastructure is not working as designed, and at minimum the fallback chain deserves a health check to confirm it is actually retrieving useful data rather than silently returning stale results.

## Caveats

All resolution figures (n=14 total, n=7 per side) are single-day snapshots. The standard threshold used throughout this project's own analysis is n≥30 per segment before drawing conclusions; today's figures are well below that for every sub-band cited. The per-band breakdowns (n=3 for both the <65% and 65–89.99% lay bands) are illustrative at best and should be treated as directional noise, not evidence. The win rate of 28.6% on 14 resolutions has a very wide confidence interval — roughly ±24 percentage points at 95% confidence — meaning today's results are consistent with a wide range of true underlying win rates. The CLV data carries a note that it reflects a 2026-09-02 generation date with zero entries logged on this day; no closing-line analysis is possible for 2026-09-21.

The 2 result-contamination flags (BUG-151: Marco Fu v On Yee Ng snooker; Legout v Ostapenkov tennis) indicate the analyzer detected and flagged in-progress or completed match results in key_facts for those markets. These were handled by the contamination guard, but their presence is a reminder that any signal touching an already-decided market in today's data should be excluded from any future back-analysis of this day's signals, not just the two flagged events.
