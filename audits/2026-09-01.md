## Caveats

Today's resolution sample is n=11 total (9 lays, 2 backs), with sub-band counts of 4, 4, and 1. At these sizes, any single-day WR or P&L figure is noise-dominated. A 27.3% daily win rate on 11 trades could be a random draw from a process with a true 50%+ win rate. The <65% lay win (1/1, +£1.90) and the back win (1/2, 50%) are particularly uninterpretable at n=1–2. The CLV sample (n=7–8) is similarly too small to assign direction. The ≥90% band result is more meaningful only because it adds to an already-long cumulative losing run — the individual day's n=4 would be unremarkable in isolation. The cache-dominance finding (250/266 cache hits) is a factual observation about the research pipeline's behaviour today, not a direct measurement of signal quality; its implication (further reduction in an already-near-zero research contribution) is an inference, not a measured figure. Any conclusion from today's data should be treated as a prompt to monitor, not a basis for live configuration changes.
