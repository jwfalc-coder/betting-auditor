Confidence: high (this is a count, not an inference). This is worth surfacing not as a finding per se — probe mode is intended — but because it means today's trading activity tells us almost nothing about the model's live edge: almost everything is being collected for future review, not acted on.

2. **Back/lay generation ratio is 25:4 (86% backs) — structurally consistent with the back-bias concern, not today's numbers alone.** The model generated 25 back signals and 4 lay signals. Established finding #1 holds that lays on selections below 65% implied probability are the only demonstrated profitable segment. With only 4 lays generated in a full day of 7 runs, and 29 signals total, the pipeline is producing very little of what the historical record says is the profitable product. This single day (n=4 lays) is too small to say the rate has changed, but 86% backs is consistent with the documented structural back-bias finding ("92% zero-boost, 23/2216 negative" in the drought diagnosis). Confidence: speculative on trend, high on the count itself.

3. **The one >=90% lay that resolved today lost** (n=1, -£0.24). The established finding #2 is that >=90% market-implied favourite lays are 1W/24L. Today contributes one more loss in that band — consistent with the finding, though at n=1 it is statistically meaningless as a standalone result. The other resolved lay was in the <65% band (the supposedly profitable segment per finding #1) and also lost (-£2.40, 0% WR). Two lays, zero wins on the day; n=2 is far too small to move any prior, but it is worth noting that even the "proven edge" band went 0/1 today. Confidence: speculative (n=2 total resolutions, n=1 per band).

4. **Gemini 429 cap exhausted in the first run, cascading through the day.** 27 rate-limit errors appeared starting at 06:04 UTC — within the opening 0600 run — with 14 research fallback activations recorded across the day. The fallback appears to have covered the gap (0 research errors, all 7 brief files returned status "ok"). Established finding #4 says web research contributes only ~0.13pp of a signal's edge on average, which suggests this degradation has minimal signal-quality impact if the fallback is functioning. However, 65% of today's research coverage came from cache (246/378 markets), so it is not clear how much of the "ok" status reflects fresh research vs. stale cached briefs. Confidence: medium on the observation, speculative on the quality-impact claim.

No circuit-breaker threshold was tripped. This is not a finding on its own — the circuit-breaker is a safety rail, not a signal — but a streak that spans multiple days without a win is consistent with the broader pattern visible in the established findings (particularly the question of whether the model has genuine edge or the historical win rate is concentrated in specific, possibly seasonal, segments now absent).

## Recommended actions

1. **Confirm whether the Gemini daily cap is being hit structurally, not just on a busy day.** 27 429-errors with fallback activations in the first run of the day suggests the quota runs out before the first research batch completes. If this is a repeated pattern (check the previous 5–7 days' logs for the same error), the options are: raise the Gemini tier/quota, shift the heaviest research batch to a lower-volume time window, or accept that fallback-quality research is now the effective primary. Given finding #4's near-zero measured research contribution, this is not urgent, but silently running on fallback every day is worth knowing about explicitly.

2. **Keep monitoring — no action warranted on signal or P&L data today.** With n=2 resolutions and 93% probe signals, there is no statistically meaningful signal in today's trading data. The back/lay generation ratio (25:4) is worth watching if it persists over the next 2–3 weeks, but a single day at any ratio is not grounds to adjust thresholds or quotas. If the probe data continues accumulating at this rate (27 probe signals/day), the segments that need ~30 resolved trades to reach a promotion/kill milestone may be closer than they appear — worth checking current segment counts before the next review cycle.

## Caveats

All P&L, win-rate, and band-specific numbers in the resolutions section rest on n=2 (one trade per band). No statistical inference is possible at this sample size. A 0% win rate on 2 lays is not evidence of deterioration; a 100% win rate would equally not be evidence of improvement. The consecutive-loss streak count reflects the pipeline's running counter, which may span multiple calendar days — it is not the same as "5 losses today." Research coverage figures (378 markets, 246 cache hits) describe volume, not quality; the audit brief does not include the content of fallback-sourced briefs, so it is not possible to assess how many of today's 29 signals were informed by genuinely fresh research vs. cached or degraded material. The 93% probe rate means almost nothing about this day's model performance can be inferred from outcomes — nearly all of it will be resolved in future tracking, not live execution.
